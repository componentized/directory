Icons

The icons the directory shows for each kind of package and item, as SVG files, for a page elsewhere that links to the directory. Each is at {{ base_url }}/_icons/{variant}/{name}.svg:

  • light and dark, in the colors of the directory's light and dark themes
  • neutral, for a page whose theme isn't known: of at least 4.5:1 contrast against both white and black

A badge's halo is cut out of the icon under it, so every variant sits on any background. Every file is 26 by 26: the icon's 16 by 16 in the middle, and a 5px margin all round for the badges that hang past its corners. An icon is in the same place and at the same size with or without badges, so a list of them lines up. A margin of -5px lays one out in the space of a 16 by 16 icon.
